Investment Opportunities Explained

  • 1. US Large Cap Equity

    Large-cap equities represent established, financially stable American companies. Investing in this sector provides exposure to the core of the US economy.

  • 2. Apple and Tesla (Individual Stocks)

    Investing in specific companies like Apple or Tesla offers the potential for high growth, but comes with idiosyncratic risk. A single company's earnings report can cause significant stock price fluctuation.

  • 3. International Equity

    Diversifying outside the US protects against domestic economic downturns. International equities carry additional risks such as currency fluctuation and geopolitical instability.

  • 4. Cash & Equivalents

    Cash and cash equivalents are crucial for liquidity. They provide a safety net for emergencies and short-term goals.

  • 5. Investment Grade Credit

    These are bonds issued by highly rated corporations or governments. They provide regular interest income and are generally lower risk than stocks.

  • 6. Sustainable Infrastructure

    Investing in infrastructure offers inflation-linked cash flows. The shift towards sustainable development creates long-term growth potential.

  • 7. Private Credit Fund IV

    Private credit involves lending directly to middle-market businesses. It can offer high yields but comes with significant liquidity risk and credit risk.
