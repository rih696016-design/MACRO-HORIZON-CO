Understanding Yields

When bond yields rise, bond prices fall. If you hold older bonds with lower interest rates, their value decreases. High yields can also make stocks less attractive, as investors can get guaranteed returns from the government without taking equity risk.

Inflation & Purchasing Power

If inflation is 4% and your savings account pays 1%, you are effectively losing 3% of your purchasing power every year. This is why merely holding cash is not a long-term wealth strategy; you must seek returns that outpace inflation.

Volatility (The VIX)

The VIX measures expected stock market volatility. A low VIX means investors are complacent; a high VIX signals fear and uncertainty. Understanding volatility helps you avoid panic-selling during temporary market dips.

Liquidity Risk

Some assets, like publicly traded stocks, can be sold instantly. Others, like real estate or private credit, take months or years to sell. Always ensure you have enough liquid assets to cover emergencies before locking money into illiquid investments.
